# Maintainers Register

## Purpose

This document is the official register of maintainers for TeachLink Mobile. It
records who holds the Maintainer role, their areas of responsibility, their
status (active or emeritus), and the date and process by which they joined or
transitioned. It provides a versioned, auditable reference for governance and
accountability.

## Scope

This register applies to people who hold the Maintainer role as defined in
[`Governance/roles/MAINTAINER.md`](../roles/MAINTAINER.md). It does not record
other roles (reviewers, moderators, etc.). Role definitions, appointment,
removal, and promotion criteria are documented in the corresponding governance
documents.

## Status Definitions

- **Active**: Currently serving as a maintainer with full decision authority
  and duties as defined in the Maintainer role.
- **Emeritus**: Former maintainer in good standing who has transitioned out of
  the role. Emeritus status may be granted following the
  [Emeritus Transition Process](../processes/EMERITUS_TRANSITION.md). Emeritus
  maintainers do not hold active voting authority but may be consulted for
  historical context.

## Update Process

Updates to this register follow the documented governance processes:

1. **Appointment**: New maintainers are recorded when appointed according to
   the [Nomination Process](../processes/NOMINATION.md) and approved by the
   existing maintainers. The date appointed and areas of responsibility must be
   recorded.
2. **Transition to Emeritus**: When a maintainer transitions to emeritus status,
   record the date of transition and link to the relevant transition record per
   the [Emeritus Transition Process](../processes/EMERITUS_TRANSITION.md).
3. **Removal/Revocation**: Changes due to removal follow the
   [Revocation Policy](../policies/REVOCATION.md), with appropriate records in
   the governance history.
4. **Proposals**: Any change to this register is proposed via a pull request
   touching only the `Governance/` folder, as specified in the Governance
   contribution rules.

## Versioning

This register is versioned with the repository. Material changes (adding,
removing, or changing status of a maintainer) must be documented in the commit
message and linked in any relevant governance records.

## Review Cadence

This register is reviewed at least annually in conjunction with the maintainer
role coverage review described in [`Governance/roles/MAINTAINER.md`](../roles/MAINTAINER.md).
