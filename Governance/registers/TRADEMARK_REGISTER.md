# Trademark Register

## Purpose

This document is the official register of trademarks, service marks, and brand
assets owned or asserted by TeachLink Mobile. It records the mark name, type,
registration status, jurisdictions, registration/filing numbers (if any),
renewal dates, and current status. It provides a clear, versioned reference to
protect and manage the project's brand.

## Scope

This register covers protected marks as defined in the
[Trademark and Brand Usage Policy](../policies/TRADEMARK.md), including the
project name, wordmark, logo, app icon, and any distinctive taglines or
campaign marks introduced in official documentation. Third-party trademarks are
not listed here.

## Status Definitions

- **Unregistered**: Common law trademark rights may exist based on use; no
  formal registration on file.
- **Pending**: Application filed and awaiting examination/approval.
- **Registered**: Formally registered with the relevant trademark office.
- **Abandoned**: Application abandoned or registration allowed to lapse.
- **Licensed/Assigned**: Rights held under license or assigned; details recorded
  in notes.

## Renewal Dates

For registered marks, renewal dates and deadlines are recorded in the table
above. Renewal actions must be tracked in advance with sufficient lead time to
maintain registration. For unregistered marks, this field is marked N/A but the
project may still track policy enforcement and usage.

## Update Process

1. **New marks**: Proposed additions to this register must be accompanied by
   rationale and approved via the governance process. Any new protected mark
   should be reflected in the [Trademark and Brand Usage Policy](../policies/TRADEMARK.md).
2. **Status changes**: Changes to registration status, jurisdiction, or renewal
   dates require maintainer review and must be recorded with the effective date
   and source (e.g., filing receipt, office action).
3. **Policy alignment**: Updates must remain consistent with the Trademark and
   Brand Usage Policy. If policy changes affect which marks are protected, the
   register should be updated accordingly.
4. **Proposals**: Changes are proposed via a pull request touching only the
   `Governance/` folder.

## Asset Location

Official brand assets referenced by marks in this register are published in the
repository's `assets/` folder and on the project website, and are versioned
alongside release artifacts as specified in the Trademark and Brand Usage
Policy.

## Versioning

This register is versioned with the repository. Material changes must be
documented and linked to the source of the change (e.g., issue/PR, filing
documentation).

## Review Cadence

This register is reviewed at least annually, or upon any change to the
Trademark and Brand Usage Policy, filing activity, or brand asset publication.
