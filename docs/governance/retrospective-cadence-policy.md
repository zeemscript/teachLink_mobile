# Retrospective Cadence Policy

This policy defines the cadence and format for project retrospectives.

## Frequency
- Sprint retrospectives: end of every 2-week sprint
- Quarterly retrospectives: end of each quarter

## Format
- What went well
- What could be improved
- Action items with owners and due dates

## Participants
- All active contributors
- At least one maintainer must be present

## Output
Action items are tracked in the project board and reviewed in the next retrospective.
