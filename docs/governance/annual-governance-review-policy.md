# Annual Governance Review Policy

This policy defines the process for the annual governance review.

## Purpose
Ensure governance documents remain relevant and effective.

## Schedule
- Conducted once per year in Q4

## Scope
- Review all governance documents for accuracy
- Assess whether policies are being followed
- Identify gaps and propose updates

## Output
A summary report is published listing changes made and rationale.

## Participants
All maintainers are required to participate.
