# Quarterly Governance Report Policy

This policy defines the format and publication process for quarterly governance reports.

## Purpose
Provide transparency into governance activities and decisions.

## Frequency
Published within 14 days of the end of each quarter.

## Contents
- Governance decisions made in the quarter
- Policy changes and rationale
- Summary of any exceptions granted
- Action items for the next quarter

## Ownership
The lead maintainer is responsible for the report.

## Distribution
Published in docs/reports/governance-YYYY-QN.md and announced in community channels.
