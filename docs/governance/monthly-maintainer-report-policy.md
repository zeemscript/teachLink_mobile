# Monthly Maintainer Report Policy

This policy defines the format and process for monthly maintainer reports.

## Purpose
Keep contributors and the community informed of project progress.

## Frequency
Published in the first week of each month covering the prior month.

## Contents
- Key decisions made
- Notable merged pull requests
- Issues triaged or closed
- Upcoming priorities

## Ownership
The lead maintainer or a designated maintainer authors the report.

## Distribution
Posted in GitHub Discussions under the Announcements category.
