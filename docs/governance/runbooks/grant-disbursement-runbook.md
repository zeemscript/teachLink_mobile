# Grant Disbursement Runbook

This runbook describes the process for disbursing grants to contributors.

## Trigger
A grant disbursement is initiated after approval by the governance committee.

## Steps
1. Verify recipient eligibility and grant amount
2. Obtain written approval from at least 2 maintainers
3. Process payment via the approved treasury tool
4. Record transaction in the financial ledger
5. Notify recipient by email
6. Publish summary in the next governance report

## Escalation
If payment fails, escalate to the treasury lead within 24 hours.
