# Community Health Report Policy

This policy defines how community health reports are created and published.

## Purpose
Track the health and sustainability of the TeachLink Mobile contributor community.

## Frequency
Published quarterly.

## Contents
- Number of active contributors
- New contributors in the period
- Issues opened and closed
- PR merge rate
- Community feedback summary

## Ownership
The community manager (or designated maintainer) authors the report.

## Distribution
Published in docs/reports/ and shared in community announcements.
