# State-of-the-Project Report Policy

This policy defines how and when state-of-the-project reports are published.

## Frequency
- Published quarterly
- Emergency reports may be published as needed

## Contents
- Summary of work completed in the period
- Open issues and blockers
- Upcoming milestones
- Community health metrics

## Ownership
The lead maintainer is responsible for drafting and publishing the report.

## Distribution
Reports are published in the repository under docs/reports/ and announced in the community forum.
